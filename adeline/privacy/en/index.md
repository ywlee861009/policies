## 4. Deleting information

- **Delete all locally stored Game data:** clear the Game address’s site data or local storage in your browser settings. This includes progress and the most recent automatic backup.

- **Starting a new game is different:** “Start again” resets progress but may keep the previous progress as an automatic backup. Clear site data for complete local deletion.

- **Exported backups:** delete them from Downloads, your file manager and any other locations where you copied them. Clearing browser storage does not delete exported files.

- **Information we hold:** we delete support records when their retention period ends or a valid deletion request applies. We separate records subject to a retention exception, explain that exception and delete them when it ends. Electronic records are removed using methods designed to prevent recovery; paper records, if any, are shredded.

We cannot remotely read or erase your device storage. For a future native app release, we will describe the actual effects of app deletion, reinstallation and operating-system backup restoration before release.

The current Game does not ask for age or date of birth and does not transmit local progress to us. Parents should contact us on a child’s behalf and avoid including unnecessary child information. This notice does not claim an exemption based on the Game’s artwork, a missing age field or a general-audience label.

- **Republic of Korea:** processing a child under 14’s personal information on consent grounds requires the legal guardian’s consent and the legally required verification.

- **United States:** where COPPA applies to information about a child under 13, collection, use or disclosure must satisfy a lawful exception or appropriate parental notice and verifiable consent. Where required, third-party disclosure for targeted advertising or other purposes needs separate parental consent. Participation will not depend on more information than reasonably necessary; child information must be retained only as long as necessary for its specific purpose. Applicable state protections for teenagers, including required sale/sharing opt-in and consent rules, also apply.

- **EEA:** the national age for a child’s own consent to consent-based online-service processing is between 13 and 16. Below the applicable age, parental authorization and reasonable verification are required. Other processing must still respect children’s interests, transparency and national rules.

If we learn of child information processed without a lawful basis, we will stop that processing and take necessary deletion and protective steps. Parents may email [ywlee861009@gmail.com](mailto:ywlee861009@gmail.com) to ask to review or delete a child’s information or stop further collection.
