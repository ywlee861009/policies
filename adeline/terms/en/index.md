## 2. Optional rewarded test ads

When you run out of ingredient energy, you can choose to request a Google rewarded test ad over an internet connection. Completing the reward requirements restores 100 units of ingredient energy. If the ad fails to load or you close it before meeting the requirements, you receive no refill. Energy refills over time without ads, and you can continue merging, moving items, and filling orders. The privacy policy explains how data is handled when you request an ad. [Privacy Policy](../../privacy/en/)

The operator provides no server storage or automatic cross-device sync for game progress. Restoring progress may be difficult if both the local save and any usable backup are lost.
