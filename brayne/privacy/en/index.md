# Trinity: Hero Expedition Privacy Policy

Kero Studio ("we") operates the Android game *Trinity: Hero Expedition* (app ID `com.kero.brayne`, the "Game").
This policy explains what personal information is processed when you play the Game, why, and how you can control it.

This English version is a translation of the [Korean policy](https://ywlee861009.github.io/policies/brayne/privacy/ko/) with additional information for users in the
European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom ([section 11](#11-additional-information-for-users-in-the-eea-and-uk)).
If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails unless the law of your country requires otherwise.

- Published: October 14, 2026
- Effective: October 14, 2026

## At a glance

| Topic | Summary |
|---|---|
| Account registration | None |
| Personal information we receive directly | None. We do not run our own servers. |
| Stored only on your device | Game progress and game settings |
| Sent to Google | Cloud save (only when signed in to Google Play Games), crash reports, advertising data |

## 1. Information we process and why

The Game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, or phone number.
The following information is processed through Google services built into the Game.

| Purpose | Information | When | Processed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saving your progress | Game progress, game settings | While you play | Your device only (never sent out) |
| Cloud save and restore | Google Play Games player ID, game progress | Only when you are signed in to Google Play Games | Google Play Games services |
| Fixing crashes and improving stability | Crash details (error, time), device model, OS version, app version, Crashlytics installation ID | When the app crashes | Firebase Crashlytics |
| Showing rewarded ads | Device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, ad interactions, your ad consent choice | When the app starts and when you watch an ad | Google AdMob (see [section 6](#6-advertising)) |

## 2. How long we keep it

| Information | Retention |
|---|---|
| Game data on your device | Until you uninstall the Game or clear its app data |
| Cloud save data | Until you delete it or delete your Google account. **It remains after you uninstall the Game.** |
| Crash reports | 90 days from collection |
| Advertising data | As set out in Google's Privacy Policy |

## 3. How information is deleted

- We do not keep your personal information on our own servers or in any other records.
- **Data on your device:** deleted immediately when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data in your device settings. Game data is also excluded from automatic device backups.
- **Cloud save data:** you can delete this game's data in your Google Play Games settings. If you need help, contact us ([section 10](#10-contact)).
- **Crash reports:** deleted automatically by Google when the retention period ends.
- We are not required by law to keep any of your personal information.

## 4. Service providers

| Provider | Service |
|---|---|
| Google LLC | Collecting, storing, and analyzing crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics |
| Google LLC | Storing cloud save data through Google Play Games services |

## 5. International transfers

To provide the services in section 4, your information is transferred outside your country.

| | Cloud save | Crash reports |
|---|---|---|
| Recipient | Google LLC | Google LLC |
| Contact | Via [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | Same |
| Countries | The United States and other countries where Google operates data centers | Same |
| When and how | Sent from time to time over an encrypted connection while you play signed in to Google Play Games | Sent over an encrypted connection after a crash |
| Information | Google Play Games player ID, game progress | Crash details, device model, OS and app version, Crashlytics installation ID |
| Purpose | Saving and restoring your progress | Fixing crashes and improving stability |
| Retention | Until you delete it or delete your Google account | 90 days from collection |
| How to refuse | Do not sign in to Google Play Games | It cannot be turned off in the Game. You can stop using the Game (uninstall it). |
| Effect of refusing | Your progress cannot be restored when you change devices or reinstall the Game | You cannot use the Game |

Google states that it protects these transfers with legally recognized safeguards such as the European Commission's
Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 6. Advertising

- The Game shows rewarded ads from Google AdMob. An ad plays only when you choose to watch one for a reward.
- To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google collects the advertising data listed in section 1 directly within the Game. This data is handled under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and may be used for personalized ads.
- The Game does not use web cookies, but your device's advertising ID serves a similar purpose.

**How to opt out**

- **Delete or reset your advertising ID:** device Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads → Delete advertising ID. Menu names may differ by device and Android version.
- **Regions where consent is required (EEA, UK, and others):** change your choice anytime in the Game under Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy options.
- You can still play the Game and watch rewarded ads if you opt out. You may see non-personalized ads instead.

## 7. Security

- **Data minimization:** no account registration and no servers of our own. We process only what the Game needs.
- **Encryption in transit:** information sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections.
- **Access control:** only we can access the consoles that show crash reports and ad statistics.
- **Backup exclusion:** game data is not copied elsewhere by automatic device backups.

## 8. Your rights

- You may ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. A parent or legal guardian may exercise these rights on behalf of a child.
- Email us ([section 10](#10-contact)) and we will respond without undue delay.
- We do not keep information that identifies you. For information Google stores, we will tell you how to delete it yourself and help you do so.
- Things you can do yourself: delete cloud save data ([section 3](#3-how-information-is-deleted)), delete your advertising ID ([section 6](#6-advertising)), uninstall the Game.

## 9. Children

The Game does not verify your age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14
(under 13 in the United States). If we learn that a child's personal information has been processed,
we will help delete it at the request of the parent or legal guardian.

## 11. Additional information for users in the EEA and UK

**Controller:** Kero Studio, [ywlee861009@gmail.com](mailto:ywlee861009@gmail.com).

**Legal bases**

- We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects.
