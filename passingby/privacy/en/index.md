# Passing By Privacy Policy

Kero Studio ("we") operates the Android app *Passing By* (app ID `com.kero.passingby`, the "App").
This policy explains what personal information is processed when you use the App, why, and how you can control it.

This English version is a translation of the [Korean policy](../) with additional information for users in the
European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom ([section 12](#eea-uk)).
If the two versions differ, the Korean version prevails unless the law of your country requires otherwise.

- Published: October 17, 2026
- Effective: October 17, 2026

## At a glance

| Topic | Summary |
|---|---|
| Account registration | None |
| Personal information we receive directly | Only your email address and message when you email us. We do not run our own servers. |
| Stored only on your device | Travel journal, app settings, list of courses you unlocked |
| Sent to Google | Payment information when you buy a course (Google Play billing); an app data backup if device backup is on |
| Ads, analytics, crash reporting | None |

<a id="data"></a>

## 1. Information we process and why

The App does not ask for or collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, or photos, and it does not use the advertising ID.

| Purpose | Information | When | Processed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Saving your journal and settings | Selected course, direction, and focus length; travel journal (arrivals, focus time, stations visited, completed courses); sound, weather, and display settings | While you use the App | Your device only (never sent out) |
| Resuming a trip in progress | Trip state, and the device boot count used to check whether the device restarted | During a trip | Your device only (never sent out) |
| Buying and restoring paid courses | Purchased product (course) ID, purchase state, purchase token | When you buy a course or the App checks your purchases | Google Play billing (see [section 5](#recipients)). The App stores only the IDs of unlocked courses on your device. |
| Confirming purchases and handling refunds | Order information provided by Google Play (order number, course purchased, price, purchase date, payment and refund status, etc.) | After a purchase | Google Play Console (accessible only to us) |
| Answering your questions | Email address, message, any attachments you send | When you email us | Our email account (Gmail) |

- Your card number and other payment details are collected and processed directly by Google Play. Neither the App nor we receive them.
- Please do not include government ID numbers, payment details, or other information we do not need in your email.

**Device backup:** if Google backup is turned on in your device settings, Android includes the on-device information above in your
Google account backup and can restore it when you reinstall the App or move to a new device. Google keeps this backup in your account; we cannot access it.

<a id="permissions"></a>

## 2. App permissions

| Permission | Used for | If you deny it |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications (optional) | Showing time remaining during a trip; arrival and end-of-break alerts | You can still use the App, but you will not be alerted on arrival while the screen is off or you are using another app |
| Vibration, foreground service | Vibrating on arrival; keeping the timer running while the screen is off | Granted without a prompt |
| Internet, billing | Connecting to Google Play billing (included with the billing library) | Granted without a prompt |

You can change the notification permission at any time in device Settings → Apps → Passing By → Notifications.

<a id="retention"></a>

## 3. How long we keep it

| Information | Retention |
|---|---|
| Data on your device | Until you uninstall the App or clear its app data |
| Device backup | Kept in your Google account under Google's backup policy. **It may remain after you uninstall the App.** |
| Purchase records | Kept by Google Play; we make no separate copy. Where the law requires us to keep transaction records, we keep them for the required period (5 years for payment and supply records and 3 years for complaint and dispute records under Korean law). |
| Support emails | Deleted without undue delay once your request is resolved. Emails about purchases or refunds are kept for 3 years to handle disputes. |

<a id="delete"></a>

## 4. How information is deleted

- We do not keep your personal information on our own servers.
- **Data on your device:** deleted immediately when you uninstall the App or clear its app data in your device settings.
- **Device backup:** you can turn backup off in device Settings → Google → Backup and delete stored backups in Google Drive (or Google One) backup management. Menu names may differ by device and Android version.
- **Purchase records:** kept by Google Play. We stop using records once their retention period ends.
- **Support emails:** permanently deleted from our mailbox when the retention period ends.

<a id="ads"></a>

## 7. Advertising and analytics

- The App contains no ads, analytics, or crash reporting tools and does not use the advertising ID.
- The App does not use web cookies and does not collect behavioral information for personalized advertising.
- If we add any of these features, we will update this policy and put any required consent in place before collection begins.

<a id="security"></a>

## 8. Security

- **Data minimization:** no account registration and no servers of our own. We process only what the App needs.
- **On-device storage:** your journal and settings are kept in app-private storage that other apps cannot access.
- **Separate payments:** payments are handled only by Google Play's billing system. Neither the App nor we can access your payment details.
- **Access control:** only we can access the Play Console, which shows order information, and our support mailbox.

<a id="rights"></a>

## 9. Your rights

- You may ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your personal information at any time. A parent or legal guardian may exercise these rights on behalf of a child.
- Email us ([section 11](#contact)) and we will respond without undue delay.
- The only information we keep is support emails and order information in the Play Console. For information Google stores, we will tell you how to delete it yourself and help you do so.
- Things you can do yourself: clear app data and delete device backups ([section 4](#delete)), turn off notifications ([section 2](#permissions)), restore purchases (in the App, 설정 (Settings) → 구매 복원 (Restore purchases)), uninstall the App.

<a id="children"></a>

## 10. Children

The App does not verify your age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14
(under 13 in the United States). If we learn that a child's personal information has been processed,
we will help delete it at the request of the parent or legal guardian.
