## 1. Using Cubie

Cubie is a puzzle app for practicing with a virtual 3×3 cube. It provides shuffle, undo and redo, a timer, solve history, and step-by-step solutions. On supported devices, you can use the camera to read the colors of a physical cube and follow its solution.

The App is free and requires no Cubie account. It currently offers no paid purchases or subscriptions. See its Google Play listing for device and operating system compatibility. Your network provider may charge for internet access under your service plan. If you need a parent or legal guardian's permission to use the App, obtain it before using the App. Statutory cancellation rights continue to apply.

## 2. Solutions and rewarded ads

You can solve the virtual cube manually without viewing ads. When you select the solver, Cubie may show a Google AdMob rewarded ad depending on the current cube state. Completing the reward condition unlocks the solution for that state. If you close an ad early, the solution may stay locked. If the ad cannot load or display, the solution may open immediately. Ads and update checks may require internet access.

Lighting, reflections, colors, and device capabilities can affect scan accuracy. Before starting a solution, check that the colors shown match your physical cube. You can correct the detected colors or scan the cube again.
