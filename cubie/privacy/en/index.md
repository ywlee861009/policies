# Cubie Privacy Policy

Kero Studio (케로스튜디오), operated by 이영우, provides the Android app **Cubie** (app ID `com.kero.cubie`). This policy explains how Cubie handles information for camera scanning, saved records, advertising, and support, and how you can manage it.

- Published and effective November 6, 2026.
- The operator is the privacy contact. Email [ywlee861009@gmail.com](mailto:ywlee861009@gmail.com) for privacy questions and requests.

## 1. Information and purposes

| Feature | Information and purpose | Where it is processed |
|---|---|---|
| Cube scanning | When you grant camera permission and start a scan, Cubie samples colors from rear-camera frames. It uses the samples to identify and display cube colors and compute a step-by-step solution. | In device memory. Cubie does not save the frames or scan results to files or upload them to a server. |
| Solve history and settings | Solve times, move counts, dates, whether the solver was used, personal bests, theme, and whether the App has requested camera permission. Cubie uses this information to show your records and statistics, remember settings, and provide permission guidance. It keeps up to 1,000 recent solve records. | In the App's private storage on your device. Android backup can include supported data if enabled. |
| Rewarded advertising | Serving and measuring ads and preventing fraud. Section 2 describes what the SDK may process. | Google AdMob (Google LLC) |
| Updates | Using device metadata, app version, and the list of installed modules and asset packs to check update availability and estimate download size, then prompting you to update when needed. | Google Play |
| Email support | The email address, message, and attachments you send us, so we can answer your question or handle your request. | Our support mailbox (Gmail, Google LLC) |

Cubie has no account registration or sign-in. We do not operate a server that receives Cubie's solve records or scan data. You can use the virtual cube with camera permission denied. To change permission, open Android **Settings → Apps → Cubie → Permissions → Camera**. Cubie does not request precise location permission.

## 2. Advertising and your choices

Cubie uses Google AdMob rewarded ads for the solver. It may preload an ad when the App starts. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can send the following data to Google for advertising, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention.

- IP address and general location estimated from it
- Interactions such as app launches, taps, and ad video views
- App and SDK performance and diagnostic information
- Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with accounts signed in on the device

The advertising SDK encrypts data in transit. Ad personalization can vary by region, device settings, Google ad settings, and your consent choices. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in Android ad settings and change Google ad personalization in My Ad Center if you use a Google account. Ads may still appear after you delete your advertising ID, and deleting it does not erase all advertising data. If an ad consent prompt is available, use its options to manage your choices. [How Google uses data from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/)

## 3. External services and international processing

- **Google AdMob** processes the advertising data above to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Ad requests and related events can be sent over encrypted connections.
- **Google Play and Android backup** handle updates, device backups, and restoration. Google Play encrypts update check data and deletes it after a fixed retention period. If you use Google backup or device transfer, Android may back up or restore supported app data. We cannot access your device backups.
- **Gmail** receives and stores the address, message, and attachments when you email us. We process that information to respond to you. You can use the App without contacting us.
- **GitHub Pages** hosts these policy and terms pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security when they open a page. We do not add our own visitor analytics or ads to these pages. [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

Google LLC may process the data for these services in the United States and other countries. Retention depends on the purpose of each service and Google's retention practices. You can turn off backup or change ad settings on your device. For information about transfers and data handling, contact us or use the contact options in Google's policy. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Google's data retention practices](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention)

## 4. Retention and deletion

- Camera frames and color samples are processed temporarily in device memory. The App releases the frames after using them for scanning and uses the applied colors for the current cube state.
- To remove local solve history, personal bests, and settings, use Android **Settings → Apps → Cubie → Storage → Clear storage**, or uninstall Cubie. Menu names vary by device. An existing backup may remain after you uninstall. Manage it separately in your Google account or device backup settings.
- We keep support emails and attachments while needed to handle the request, then delete them. If the law requires retention or the information is needed for an ongoing dispute, we keep it only for the required period and then delete it from the mailbox.
- The advertising SDK and other Google services follow Google's retention practices. Use [Google Account Data & Privacy](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy) to manage data and activity available in your Google account.

We process scans on the device, keep app records in app-private storage, and restrict access to the support mailbox.

## 5. Your rights and how to contact us

You or your legal representative can email us to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information that we process. Where applicable, you can also withdraw consent, object to processing, or request data portability. We may verify your identity or your representative's authority as necessary before responding under applicable law. A parent or guardian with questions about a child's data can use the same contact address.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the UK, we rely on performance of a contract where processing is necessary to provide app features you request. For support, we rely on contract performance or our legitimate interest in responding to requests and resolving disputes. We rely on legal obligation for required retention. Where consent is required for advertising, that processing is based on consent and you may withdraw it. You can lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority.

In Korea, you can also contact the [Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee](https://www.kopico.go.kr) at 1833-6972 or the [Personal Information Infringement Report Center](https://privacy.kisa.or.kr) at 118.
