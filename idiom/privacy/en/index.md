# DoIt! 사자성어 Privacy Policy

Kero Studio, operated by Youngwoo Lee, operates **DoIt! 사자성어**, an Android idiom learning app (app ID `com.kero.idiom`). This policy explains how the app handles learning records, advertising, analytics, crash reports, and support requests.

- Published and effective November 6, 2026.
- For privacy inquiries and rights requests, contact Youngwoo Lee at [ywlee861009@gmail.com](mailto:ywlee861009@gmail.com).

## 1. Information and purposes

You can use the app without creating an account or signing in. Kero Studio does not operate a server that receives your learning records. The app does not ask you for a name, phone number, or payment information and does not request precise location, contacts, camera, or microphone permissions.

| Feature | Information and purpose | Where it is processed |
|---|---|---|
| Learning records and settings | The app saves how often each idiom was shown and answered correctly, collected idioms, quiz totals and scores, study dates and streaks, experience points, level, combos, reminder settings, and the study materials version. It uses these to select review questions, show progress and statistics, and remember settings. | App-specific storage on your device. Android backup or device transfer can include supported data. |
| Study reminders | If you allow notifications, the app schedules and displays study reminders on the device. You can change them in the app's profile settings or in Android notification settings. | Your device |
| Study material updates | The app requests public idiom materials and version information over HTTPS. GitHub may receive the IP address and request details needed to deliver those files. The request does not contain your learning records. | GitHub |
| Rewarded ads | Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, interactions such as launches, taps and ad views, performance and diagnostic information, and identifiers such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers. These support ad delivery, measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. | Google AdMob (Google LLC) |
| Usage analytics | Google may process usage events such as app opens, sessions and screen views, the app instance ID and, depending on the environment, the advertising ID, app, device and operating system information, language, and approximate country or region. We use usage reports to understand and improve the app. | Google Analytics for Firebase (Google LLC) |
| Crash reporting | Google may process error and crash logs, stack traces, app and device state, installation identifiers, and some events preceding a crash. We use reports to reproduce and fix problems. | Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) |
| Support email | We receive your email address, message, and any attachments you send to answer requests and handle complaints. Please share only information needed for your request. | Kero Studio's support inbox (Gmail, Google LLC) |

## 2. Ads and external services

- Choosing **Show Hint** may open a rewarded ad. The app may preload an ad before you choose it. SDK data is encrypted in transit. You can delete or reset the advertising ID in Android's ad settings and manage Google account ad personalization in My Ad Center. These settings affect ad personalization and do not control the separate analytics or crash reporting services. [How Google uses information from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) · [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/)
- Opening Naver Hanja Dictionary from the library sends the selected idiom as a search query to the external service. Naver handles connection information under its own policy. [Naver Privacy Policy](https://policy.naver.com/policy/privacy.html)
- GitHub serves study materials and GitHub Pages hosts this policy and the terms. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. Kero Studio does not install separate advertising or analytics on these policy pages. [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 3. Recipients and international processing

Google LLC processes data for ads, analytics, crash reporting, support email, and Android backup if you choose to use it. GitHub serves the public study materials and hosts the policy website. They may process the information described in sections 1 and 2 in the United States and other countries where they operate infrastructure. Ads, analytics, and diagnostic data may be transmitted over encrypted connections when related requests or events occur. Study material requests and policy page visits use HTTPS. Email is transmitted when you send a message, and backups follow your device settings. Kero Studio cannot access your device's Google backup.

See section 4 for retention and deletion. You can choose whether to send us an email or use device backup. Turning off backup may affect your ability to restore future app data. See sections 2 and 5 for advertising settings and privacy rights. Contact us or the relevant provider with questions about international processing. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) · [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## 4. Retention, deletion, and safeguards

- Learning records and settings stay on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data under **Android Settings → Apps → DoIt! 사자성어 → Storage → Clear data**. Existing backups may remain after you uninstall. Use your device or Google account backup controls to remove them separately.
- We keep support emails and attachments for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and then delete them from our mailbox. If a law requires us to keep them or a dispute is pending, we keep them for as long as that purpose requires.
- Crashlytics retains crash stacks, diagnostic materials, and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting deletion. Analytics deletes user-level and event-level data according to the Analytics property's retention settings. Aggregated reports may be retained separately. Ads and other Google services follow their respective retention rules. Clearing app data on the device does not remove previously transmitted information from those services. [Firebase retention](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) · [Analytics data retention](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196) · [Google data retention](https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention)
- We store learning records in app-specific storage and restrict access to the support mailbox and SDK administration consoles.

## 5. Your rights

You or your legal representative can email us to request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of processing. Depending on applicable law, you may also withdraw consent, object to processing, or request data portability. We may verify your identity or authority to act before processing a request. Parents and guardians can contact us at the same address about a child's data.

Where EEA or UK privacy law applies, providing requested app features may rely on performance of a contract. Support and service security may rely on legitimate interests where permitted, and required retention may rely on a legal obligation. Where consent is required for ads or analytics, the applicable processing relies on that consent and you can withdraw it. You can lodge a complaint with your competent data protection authority.

In South Korea, you can also contact the [Personal Information Infringement Report Center](https://privacy.kisa.or.kr) (118) or the [Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee](https://www.kopico.go.kr) (1833-6972).
